Silent Timer
Privacy Policy

Effective date: 9 October 2026

In short: Silent Timer does not ask you to create an account, and the developer does not collect your name, contacts, location, files or messages. Your timer settings stay on your phone. The app shows ads through Google AdMob, and Google may use your device's advertising ID for that purpose (see section 5).

1. Who we are

Silent Timer (“the app”) is an Android app developed by Talha Shahbaz (kinggtech) (“we”, “us”). The app switches your phone to silent mode for a time you choose, then restores normal sound mode when the timer ends.

If you have any question about this policy, write to talhashahbaz848@gmail.com.

2. Information we collect

We do not collect, store or send any personal information to our own servers. We do not run any servers for this app, and the app has no sign-up, login or user profile.

The app saves the following on your device only, so that the timer keeps working if the app is closed:

  • whether a timer is currently running,
  • the timer’s start time, end time and duration.

This data never leaves your phone. It is deleted when the timer finishes, when you cancel it, or when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

3. Permissions the app uses

The app asks only for the permissions needed to do its one job.

PermissionWhy the app needs it
Do Not Disturb access
ACCESS_NOTIFICATION_POLICY		 Lets the app change your phone’s sound mode to silent when the timer starts and back to normal when it ends. The app only changes the sound mode. It does not read your notifications or calls.
Notifications
POST_NOTIFICATIONS		 Shows the “Silent Mode Active” notification while a timer runs and the “Silent Timer Ended” notification, with a Restart button, when it finishes.
Exact alarms
SCHEDULE_EXACT_ALARM, USE_EXACT_ALARM		 Makes sure the timer ends at the exact time you chose, even when the app is in the background.

You can withdraw any of these permissions at any time in your phone’s Settings. The app will not be able to run timers without them.

4. How we use information

The on-device timer data described above is used only to run the timer and show its notifications. We do not use it for any other purpose, and we do not sell, rent or share it.

5. Advertising and third-party services

The app uses Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK) to show an ad when the app is opened. To serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and keep the service secure, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • your device’s advertising ID,
  • your IP address and approximate location derived from it,
  • device and app information (device model, Android version, app version, language),
  • ad interactions (ad views and clicks) and diagnostics.

We do not receive this information in a form that identifies you. Google handles it under its own policies:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services

You can reset your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalization on your phone under Settings → Google → Ads (the exact path can vary by device).

The app uses no other third-party analytics, crash-reporting or tracking service.

6. Data sharing

We do not sell your data. The only third party involved is Google, through AdMob, as described in section 5. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

7. Data retention and deletion

Timer data on your device is removed automatically when a timer ends or is cancelled. To remove everything the app has stored, uninstall the app or clear its storage in Android Settings. Because we do not hold any data about you on our own servers, there is nothing for us to delete on our side. For data held by Google for advertising, see the Google links above.

8. Children’s privacy

The app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information through the app, contact us and we will help.

9. Security

Because the app keeps its data on your device and sends none of it to us, the main protection is your phone’s own security. We follow standard Android practices to keep the app’s local storage private to the app.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. When we do, we will change the effective date at the top of this page. Please check back occasionally. Continuing to use the app after a change means you accept the updated policy.
